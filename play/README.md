# Fuse Frenzy browser playtest

[Play the game](https://downandoutstudios.github.io/play/)

Ten campaign levels, Armourdrillo between Levels 5 and 6, and the Furnace King finale. Play independently without an account, download or Unreal Editor.

Choose Arcade Campaign, Time Attack, Hardcore or Frenzy Marathon using the illustrated panels. Arcade has three continues and local checkpoints. Hardcore starts with one life and cannot gain extra lives. Time Attack records completed run times. Marathon carries lives and score through the full campaign without continues.

Controls: WASD/arrows move, Space places a bomb, Q/E rotates, Escape pauses. Sound starts off. Defeat enemies, then walk into the active gate.

Eight power-ups: Speed, Bomb Capacity, Blast Range, Extra Life, Shield, Bomb Kick, Freeze and Double Bomb Push. Freeze stops enemies and boss attacks for five seconds while bomb timers continue. Double Bomb Push pushes bombs up to two cells, stopping at obstacles. HUD icons use the same new 3D models as pickups.

Arcade now includes Hidden Room 1, Workshop Pressure Lock, after Level 2: seat both weights, bomb the circuit crate, collect the first of five matching wedges and walk through the activated gate to Level 3. R resets a stuck puzzle while keeping recovered pieces. Room 2, Steam Routing Chamber, follows Level 4. Press F beside valves: source, branch and turbine ON; bleed OFF. Avoid active vents, recover the second wedge and use the gate to return to Level 5. Other rooms are being added individually after testing. Multiplayer and Teegwa are excluded. This browser port is separate from the Unreal project and Windows game.
