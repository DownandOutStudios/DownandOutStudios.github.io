Inside the workshop

Fuse Frenzy

Brass, steam and a very short fuse. Find your way through the maze, outsmart clockwork bots and uncover the secrets hiding beneath the crates.

Make your escape

Place bombs, clear wooden crates and leave yourself a safe route. Metal blocks stop your blast.

Build your advantage

Uncover power-ups, defeat the bots and walk into the activated teleport gate to reach the next arena.

Face the Furnace King

Battle through ten arenas, defeat Armourdrillo between Levels 5 and 6, and face the Furnace King in the finale.

Currently in pre-alpha. Artwork previews may differ from the playable build.
